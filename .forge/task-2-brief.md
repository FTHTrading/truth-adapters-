
Routing: FULL FORGE; Phase 6 not crossed. Push remains blocked on F-1.

## What

Freeze the wire formats (`truth-record-v1`, `truth-attestation-v1`), make
`labels` and `limitations` mandatory on every emitted attestation, add
append-only corrections as references, publish reproducible positive and
negative test vectors, add discovery routes that disclose test/dry-run mode,
and prove a fresh copy installs and passes from nothing.

## Why

A stranger must be able to validate a record with the published schema and
verifier, offline, and get the same verdict we get. Until the formats are
frozen, every consumer is coupled to our source.

## In scope

- `docs/schema/*.schema.json` (JSON Schema 2020-12) + structural validation
  in the verifier without a schema library.
- `labels` object: `{mode, status, anchoring, review}` on manifest and responses.
- `correction` adapter: payload references a prior entry hash; `/verify`
  lists `referenced_by`. The referenced entry is never changed.
- `vectors/`: generated by `scripts/vectors.ts` with a committed TEST-ONLY key
  and a fixed clock; a test regenerates and diffs byte-for-byte.
- Routes: `/status.json`, `/pricing.json`, `/openapi.json`, `/llms.txt`,
  `/.well-known/x402`, `/.well-known/security.txt` (404 unless
  `SECURITY_CONTACT` is set).
- `scripts/fresh-check.ps1`: copy tree without node_modules to temp, install
  frozen, typecheck, test.

## Out of scope

Push, deploy, mainnet, external anchoring, Moltbook, pricing changes, renames.

## Acceptance

1. Every vector validates against the schema (ajv, tests only) and against the
   verifier's built-in structural checks; verdicts match `expected.json`.
2. Regenerating vectors produces identical bytes.
3. Every attestation and the manifest carry `labels` and `limitations`; tests assert it.
4. A correction never alters the referenced entry; `/verify` shows the link.
5. `/.well-known/x402` says `DRY_RUN` unless mode is `live`.
6. Fresh-copy check passes.
