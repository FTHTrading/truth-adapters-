## Incident during ship (recorded, not repaired)

To exercise the append-only triggers I inserted a probe row (`seq 999999`,
`hash "probe-hash"`) into D1 `truth-ledger` (`9b2b71c4-…`) and then attempted
UPDATE and DELETE. Both aborted with `ledger is append-only:
SQLITE_CONSTRAINT_TRIGGER`, which is the property under test. The probe row is
therefore permanent, and it is not a valid genesis entry: a ledger built on
that database could never verify from `seq 0`. HEARTH Rule 10 applies; nothing
was repaired or deleted. The gateway is pointed at a fresh database
`truth-ledger-v1` instead. `truth-ledger` is abandoned, holds one row of mine,
and may be deleted by the founder. Class-level fix: production ledgers are
never probed; the trigger test moves to a local D1 (`wrangler d1 execute --local`)
and `/health` will report whether the first row is `seq 0` (minor finding).
